fps: rebake the stale 4:00 arm-clip batch with correct weapon pairing

MagCheck's magazine floated detached from the hands. CB_ik_hand_l carries
CHILD_OF -> AK:magazine at influence 1.0, so the left arm is baked against the
weapon action playing alongside it -- and all 11 clips written at 4:00:42
predate the A_FP_x -> A_W_x pairing rule, exported while the .blend was saved
with the weapon parked on A_W_Tac_Reload.

Why only Mag_Check ever showed it: A_W_Tac_Reload's magazine starts moving
around frame 44, and every other stale clip's range ends at 46 or earlier
(Draw 0..46, Fire 0..36, Holster 0..28, IdleToSprint 0..16, Regrip 0..40,
Sprint/SprintToIdle/Walk 0..44). There the wrong pairing was latent. Mag_Check
runs 0..260, deep into the swap. Tac_Reload was correct by accident -- it was
baked against the very action it pairs with.

A/B proof (same action, same 0..260 range, same meshes=0, only the paired
weapon action differing), hand_l world path:

  shipped (as found)               1085.7 mm
  rebaked vs A_W_Tac_Reload        1086.3 mm   delta   0.6
  rebaked vs A_W_Mag_Check         1062.9 mm   delta -22.8

Pairing alone swings the hand 23.4 mm and the shipped file lands on the
A_W_Tac_Reload bake to within noise. This corrects FPS_ANIMATION_SYSTEM.md §2,
which previously claimed Mag_Check "measured identical under both pairings".

Changes:
- Re-exported 10 stale arm clips via work/export_clip.py with correct pairing:
  Mag_Check, Draw, Fire, Holster, IdleToSprint, Regrip, Sprint, SprintToIdle,
  Tac_Reload, Walk.
- meshes=0 for all of them: clip FBXs are channel-only and geometry belongs to
  the ADS armsModel. meshes=1 had produced 267 nodes plus stray Mesh.001 /
  Mesh.003 meshes and doubled the file size; meshes=0 gives 265 nodes / 0
  meshes, an exact structural match to the clips that already work
  (Inspect/Idle/Melee), and leaves motion byte-identical.
- hand_l deltas vs what shipped: Mag_Check -22.8 mm (lowerarm_l -67.0 mm),
  everything else <=3.2 mm. The nine no-op rows were rebaked anyway so the
  batch is one exporter run in one format.
- ADS deliberately NOT touched: it is the armsModel (geometry, bind pose and
  node tree every clip attaches to) and its animation is never played, so
  rebaking it was the only move here that could break everything. Idle, Aim,
  Empty_Reload, Inspect and Melee were already correct.

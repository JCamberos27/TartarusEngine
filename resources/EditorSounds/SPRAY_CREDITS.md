# Spray sounds: source and license

`SprayStart.wav`, `SprayLoop.wav`, `SprayEnd.wav` and `SprayDrip.wav` are cut by `tools/audio/build_spray.py` (rerun it, don't hand edit)
from one recording on [Freesound](https://freesound.org), released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain,
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>). No attribution is required and these edits are CC0 too; we credit
the recordist anyway. (`LICENSE.md` is generated by `build_editor_ui.py` and covers the other files.)

| File | Cut from "Aerosol spray.wav" by C-V, <https://freesound.org/s/516815/> |
|---|---|
| `SprayStart.wav` | 0.60-0.95 s (the first burst's attack), 80 ms fade-out |
| `SprayLoop.wav` | 4.35-6.05 s (the middle burst), last 300 ms crossfaded into the head: a seamless 1.40 s loop |
| `SprayEnd.wav` | 5.95-6.45 s (the middle burst's release), 40 ms fade-in, 30 ms fade-out |
| `SprayDrip.wav` | the loudest 110 ms inside 0.60-0.95 s (the first burst's attack): a short wet tick; 4 ms fade-in, 70 ms fade-out |

All four: L+R folded to mono, 48 kHz 16-bit, one shared gain (+6.5 dB) that puts the loop's peak at -3 dBFS.
The original (48 kHz 24-bit stereo, downloaded 2026-10-21) belongs on the shared Drive at `Raw\Audio\audio-src\spray`.
